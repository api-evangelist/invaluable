# Invaluable

> Invaluable is the world's leading online marketplace for fine art, antiques and collectibles (founded 1986). It aggregates live and timed auctions from 5,000+ premier auction houses across 53 countries, listing 260,000+ items and an archive of 66+ million price records. Invaluable also operates AuctionZip and a Private Label / Connect white-label platform, and exposes a partner-only Catalog Upload API for auction-management software vendors. Backed by Insight Partners.

## APIs
- [Catalog Upload API](https://www.invaluable.com/inv/apiinfo/): Partner-only API for auction-management software companies to add, edit, and delete lots in timed and live sales on Invaluable, AuctionZip and Private Label / Connect sites. Access is provisioned through Invaluable's software partners (e.g. Wavebid); onboarding includes sandbox mapping. No public OpenAPI specification, developer portal, or SDK is published.
